# Name ideas

The app reads your stars on the phone (Western chart, Chinese zodiac and Four Pillars, blood type,
palm) and, if you opt in, tells you when a compatible person is standing nearby, so the two of you
can swipe, match for dating or friendship, and chat. A good name should hint at **two people
meeting under the same sky**, read well as an App Store / Play title, and not collide with an
existing astrology or dating app.

Working name: **Conjunction**. Tagline: *Your stars, nearby.*

Conflict checks were run on **2026-11-03** (see [How the checks were done](#how-the-checks-were-done)).
They are a first screen, not legal clearance.

## Recommendation

| Rank | Name | Store title (≤ 30) | Why | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Conjunction** | `Conjunction: Astrology Dating` | Exact astrological meaning: two bodies meeting in one part of the sky, here two people in one place. Gives the app its vocabulary for free ("It's a Conjunction!", the ☌ glyph icon, "Conjunctions nearby"). Real word, easy to say and spell. | **Low.** No astrology, dating or social app of that name on either store; the only store hits are grammar apps and a writing tool. No live CONJUNCTION mark found for software or dating services in the aggregators (USPTO itself not yet searched by hand, see below). It is a dictionary word, so protection is narrower; pair it with the ☌ logo. |
| **2** | **Close Orbit** | `Close Orbit: Astrology Match` | Plain-English, romantic, and literally what the app detects (someone in your orbit, close enough for Bluetooth to find them). Works for friends as well as dating. | **Low.** Nothing on either store; no CLOSE ORBIT trademark found. Several pending ORBIT marks in class 9 (Orbit App LLC, Orbit Technologies, Orbit Live), so a lawyer should look at ORBIT-family conflicts before filing. |
| **3** | **Kindred Sky** | `Kindred Sky: Astrology Match` | Warm and human; "kindred spirits" under one sky. Leans into friendship and dating equally, softer than a pure dating brand (helps with App Store 4.3, which treats dating as a saturated category). | **Low–medium.** No app or mark called Kindred Sky. KINDRED alone is registered for mobile software (Kindred, Inc.) and several "Kindred" apps exist on Play, so the two-word mark must be used together. |

Honourable mentions: **Magpie Bridge** (the Qixi legend: magpies bridge the Milky Way so the star
lovers Altair and Vega can meet; clear on both stores, the strongest dating-and-Chinese-zodiac name —
see `docs/NAMES_DATING.md`) and **Syzygy** (the most distinctive word, but hard to spell or search
for, and several live SYZYGY marks exist; see below).

Dating names draw more enforcement than astrology names: Match Group (Tinder, Hinge) has sued over
sound-alike names and the swipe gesture, so keep **match**, **swipe**, **tinder** and **hinge** out of
the name. The closest competitor in concept is **Trine: Astrology & Match**
([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/trine-astrology-match/id6757916010)); avoid "Trine". Note
that `conjunction.app` is already registered to someone else (per `docs/NAMES_DATING.md`), so pick a
domain such as `getconjunction.app` or a path on the owner's site.

More romance-leaning ideas (Magpie Bridge, Venus Hour, Celestial Crush, Qixi, …) and their
conflict checks are in **`docs/NAMES_DATING.md`**, written in parallel for SPEC 5b.

If the owner prefers a pure dating sound, **Stars Aligned** and **Starcrossed** are the obvious
phrases, and both are taken (below) — which is a good reason to stay with Conjunction.

## All ideas (30)

### The sky's meeting words (astronomy and astrology terms for bodies coming together)

| Name | Rationale |
|---|---|
| **Conjunction** | Two planets at the same longitude; two people at the same spot. The working name. |
| **Syzygy** | Three bodies in a line (Sun–Earth–Moon at an eclipse); a famously beautiful word for alignment. |
| **Close Orbit** | What the Nearby radar actually finds: someone in your orbit right now. |
| **Perigee** | The Moon's closest approach to Earth; "closest approach" as a brand for proximity. |
| **Ingress** | A planet entering a sign; a person entering your space. Crisp, but sounds like a network term. |
| **Occult** | When one body passes in front of another. Evocative but reads as "the occult"; rejected. |
| **Stellium** | Three or more planets clustered in one sign: a crowd of kindred people. Taken (below). |
| **Synastria** | Greek root of "synastry", the art of comparing two charts. Coined-sounding and ownable. |

## How the checks were done

- **App Store:** Apple's public iTunes Search API (`itunes.apple.com/search?entity=software&country=us`),
  50 results per name, matched on the title.
- **Google Play:** the public Play search page (`play.google.com/store/search?c=apps&gl=US`). Play's
  server-rendered page returns only the first ~30 results, so a small app far down the ranking
  could be missed.
- **Trademarks:** web searches of USPTO data mirrored by Justia, Markinton and TrademarkDir. **The
  USPTO's own search (https://tmsearch.uspto.gov) is a JavaScript application that could not be
  queried from here**, and the mirrors can be months out of date. Before committing to a name the
  owner should:
  1. Search https://tmsearch.uspto.gov for the chosen name (and its phonetic variants) in classes 9
     (downloadable software), 42 (SaaS), 45 (online dating / social introduction) and 41
     (entertainment / horoscopes), plus EUIPO (https://www.tmdn.org/tmview/) for the EU and UKIPO.
  2. Search both stores by hand in the main target countries.
  3. Check the domains (e.g. `conjunction.app`, `getconjunction.com`) and social handles.
  4. Consider filing an intent-to-use application in classes 9 and 45 once the name is chosen.

## Changing the name

The name lives in exactly one place per platform (Android `strings.xml` `app_name`, iOS
`CFBundleDisplayName`), plus the store copy in `store/` (`title.txt`, `name.txt`, descriptions and
keywords — rerun `store/tools/check_lengths.py` after editing) and the legal documents in `legal/`.
The package / bundle id `com.metavirtuoso.conjunction` can stay even if the display name changes,
but it is permanent once the first build is uploaded.
