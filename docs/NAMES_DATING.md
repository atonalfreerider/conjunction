# Name ideas, dating edition

This is a companion to `docs/NAMES.md`. SPEC 5b adds dating: gender and seeking, swipe-to-accept
match cards, and chat after a mutual right swipe. This file lists names that lean toward romance
and checks each one for obvious conflicts. The working name stays **Conjunction**. Any name here
can be combined with a store subtitle, such as "Conjunction: Astrology Dating".

How the checks were done (2026-11-03):

- **App Store:** the iTunes Search API (`itunes.apple.com/search?entity=software&country=us`).
  A name counts as a hit when the app title contains the candidate.
- **Google Play:** the public US search page (`play.google.com/store/search?q=…&c=apps`), with
  titles and package ids taken from the HTML.
- **Trademarks:** web searches of USPTO data mirrored by aggregators (Justia, Markinton,
  TrademarkDir, GleanMark). USPTO's own Trademark Search (tmsearch.uspto.gov) is a JavaScript app
  that could not be queried from here. **These checks are knockout screens, not clearance.** Before
  committing to a name, run a search at tmsearch.uspto.gov in Class 9 (downloadable software) and
  Class 45 (online dating and social introduction services), plus EUIPO and the WIPO Global Brand
  Database, and have a trademark attorney review the result.

Dating names attract more trademark enforcement than astrology names. Match Group (Tinder, Hinge)
has sued over sound-alike names and over the swipe gesture (Tinder v. 3nder; Match Group v.
Muzmatch). Stay clear of **match**, **swipe**, **tinder**, **bumble** and **hinge** in the name.

## The ideas

### East meets West: myths of lovers among the stars
| # | Name | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Magpie Bridge** | In the Qixi legend, magpies form a bridge across the Milky Way once a year so the star lovers Altair and Vega can meet. That is a Chinese-zodiac-flavoured way of saying "we bring two people together". |
| 2 | **Red Thread** | The East Asian "red thread of fate" ties two people who are destined to meet. |
| 3 | **Qixi** | China's star-lovers' festival (the "Chinese Valentine's Day"). It is short, but non-Chinese speakers may not know how to say it. |
| 4 | **Altair & Vega** | The two star lovers themselves. It is elegant, but long for a store title. |
| 5 | **Weaver Star** | Vega, the Weaver Girl of the Qixi tale. |

Direct competitors in astrology dating, for positioning: Struck, NUiT, Align (Align Astrology LLC),
Stars Align, Sun Moon Rising, Astroverse, Astribes, Moonz, and **Trine: Astrology & Match**
([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/trine-astrology-match/id6757916010)). Trine is in
Lifestyle and is the closest in concept, so do not use "Trine" in any form. **Starcrossed** and
**Stellium** are also taken by astrology apps with romance features (see `NAMES.md`).

## Recommendation for the dating positioning

1. **Conjunction**, with the store title "Conjunction: Astrology Dating" (29 characters). The
   name still fits, since it means two bodies meeting. It is free of conflicts in both stores, and
   the subtitle tells people about dating, which Play and App Store search both read.
2. **Magpie Bridge.** The only name here that is both clear and romantic, and it ties into the
   Chinese zodiac theme. Its weakness is that Western users will not get the meaning without the
   tagline "Where star-crossed lovers meet".
3. **Celestial Crush.** Clear on both stores and immediately understood as dating. It drops the
   proximity and East-meets-West ideas.

Before choosing, the owner should run a USPTO search in Classes 9 and 45 for the top pick and
register the matching domain. A DNS check on 2026-11-03 (no NS record, which suggests but does
not prove a domain is unregistered) found `magpiebridge.app`, `celestialcrush.app` and
`celestialcrush.com` apparently free. `magpiebridge.com` is registered (NameBright DNS).
