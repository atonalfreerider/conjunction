# backend/ — NOT USED by the serverless build

> **Not used (serverless build).** Since the owner's decision of 2026-11-03 (SPEC section 5c),
> Conjunction has **no server**. Phones find each other and exchange match cards and end-to-end
> encrypted chat directly over Bluetooth Low Energy. The Android and iOS apps do not include
> Firebase, push notifications or the TypeSafe server moderation, and nothing in this folder is
> deployed.
>
> This folder is kept only as a record of the earlier server design (SPEC sections 5, 5b, 6 and
> "Backend (Firebase)"). Do not deploy it, do not add `google-services.json` /
> `GoogleService-Info.plist` to the apps, and do not describe it in the legal or store documents:
> `legal/` and `store/` describe the serverless build only.
