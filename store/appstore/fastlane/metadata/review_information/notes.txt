Conjunction is an 18+ astrology app with optional, proximity-based matching for dating or friendship. It has NO servers and NO accounts: phones find each other over Bluetooth Low Energy and exchange match cards and end-to-end encrypted chat directly. The developer receives no user data.

SIGN-IN: none exists.

READINGS (no network needed): complete onboarding (18+ age gate, terms, waiver, privacy) and enter any birth date, time and place. Chart, Chinese zodiac / Four Pillars and blood-type readings are computed on the device; horoscopes are written by Apple Foundation Models where Apple Intelligence is on, otherwise by a built-in template library. Palm: Readings > Palm, photograph an open hand; the photo stays on the device. Every reading has "Report this reading".

Swipe right to open a sample chat; try Report and Block from the card or chat menu.

TWO-DEVICE TEST: install on two iPhones (or an iPhone and an Android phone), complete onboarding with compatible settings (e.g. A: man seeking women, B: woman seeking men, both Looking for: Dating), turn on Nearby on both and allow Bluetooth and notifications. Keep the phones close together, with the iPhone app open. Both show the match animation; swipe right on both to open the encrypted chat. Screen recording of this test: [DEMO VIDEO URL].

BLUETOOTH AND BACKGROUND MODES: Nearby is off by default and starts only when the user turns it on after an explanation of exactly what is sent. UIBackgroundModes bluetooth-central and bluetooth-peripheral keep it working while the phone is in a pocket. The app uses no location and asks for no location permission; no distance is shown. Known iOS limitation, explained in the app: a backgrounded iPhone can be found by other iPhones but not by Android phones.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT (1.2): no messaging is possible before both people accept each other. The receiving iPhone filters incoming content on the device: photos are checked with Sensitive Content Analysis when enabled, otherwise blurred until tapped; incoming messages are screened by the on-device model and flagged ones are hidden behind a warning. Block and Report are on every match card, friend and chat. Report blocks the person immediately and opens a pre-filled email to our support team; a person reads every report within 24 hours and escalates threats and child-safety reports to the authorities. Because chats are end-to-end encrypted with no server, we cannot read them; we only see what a user sends us in a report. Strangers are not paired at random: both must opt in, be within Bluetooth range at the same time, and fit each other's stated intentions. Contact: themetavirtuoso@gmail.com. Terms and Community Guidelines: Profile > Settings > Privacy & legal.

PRIVACY: App Privacy is "Data Not Collected" because nothing reaches us or any partner; cards and chats go phone to phone. Profile > Settings > Privacy & legal > Delete all my data wipes the phone.

WHAT IS DIFFERENT (4.3): no servers at all; readings generated entirely on device; Western natal chart, Chinese Four Pillars, blood type and on-device palm analysis combined; matching only between people physically near each other at the same moment, with no browsing of profiles.

All readings and scores are for entertainment.
