Conjunction is an 18+ astrology app with optional, proximity-based matching for dating or friendship.

SIGN-IN: none needed. The app creates an anonymous account when you set up Nearby; Sign in with Apple (and Google) is optional, for restoring the account.

READINGS (no account, no network): complete onboarding (18+ age gate, terms, waiver, privacy), enter any birth date, time and place. Chart, Chinese zodiac / Four Pillars and blood-type readings are computed on the device; horoscopes are written by Apple Foundation Models where Apple Intelligence is on, otherwise by a built-in template library. Palm: Readings > Palm, photograph an open hand; the photo stays on the device.

NEARBY, MATCH CARD AND CHAT WITH ONE DEVICE: Nearby needs two members within about 50 m. So that you can test with one device, the demo companion "Aurora (App Review Demo)" is enabled on our server during review:
1. In onboarding or Profile set the display name to AppReview, gender and seeking as you like, Looking for: Both.
2. Turn on Nearby (Settings or the Nearby tab), allow location While Using the App and notifications.
3. Within about a minute the match animation plays and Aurora's swipe card appears. Swipe right (or tap Accept). Aurora always accepts, so the chat opens; she answers with one fixed demo message.
4. From the card or the chat menu try Report, Block, Unmatch, Pause Nearby. Profile > Delete account erases the account.
Aurora is only matched with accounts named AppReview and is switched off after review.

TWO-DEVICE TEST: install on two iPhones, complete onboarding with compatible settings (e.g. A: man seeking women, B: woman seeking men, both Looking for: Dating) and turn on Nearby on both, side by side. Both get the match notification; swipe right on both to open the chat. A screen recording of this test: [DEMO VIDEO URL].

LOCATION: When In Use only; we never ask for Always. Background location updates are started only from the foreground when the user turns Nearby on, with the blue indicator shown (UIBackgroundModes: location), so matches arrive while the phone is in a pocket. Exact location is never shown to other users; the server keeps only the latest position for 10 minutes.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT (1.2): display names, bios and every chat message are screened by automated moderation before anyone sees them; profile photos are checked by image moderation and are not shown until approved. Report and Block are on every match card, in every chat and on every profile; reports reach a human review queue, and we act on them within 24 hours. No chat is possible before both people accept each other; strangers are not paired at random (both must opt in, be within 50 m, and fit each other's stated intentions). Contact: themetavirtuoso@gmail.com. Terms and Community Guidelines are linked in Settings > Legal.

WHAT IS DIFFERENT (4.3): readings are generated entirely on device; we combine Western natal chart, Chinese Four Pillars, blood type and on-device palm analysis; and matching is only between people physically near each other at the same moment, with no browsing of profiles.

All readings and scores are for entertainment.
