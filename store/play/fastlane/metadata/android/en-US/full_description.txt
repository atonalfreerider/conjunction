In astrology, a conjunction is two planets meeting in the same part of the sky. Conjunction is the app for two people meeting in the same place.

Read your stars in depth, privately on your phone. Then, only if you choose, let Conjunction tell you when someone compatible is standing nearby, for dating or for friendship.

YOUR BIRTH CHART
• Enter your birth date, exact birth time and birthplace. Conjunction computes your chart on the phone: Sun, Moon, Mercury to Pluto, Ascendant, Midheaven, the twelve houses and the major aspects.
• Watch your chart wheel draw itself in gold leaf, in a hand-crafted Celestial Art Nouveau style.
• Don't know your birth time? You still get your planets; houses and Rising sign wait until you add it.

HOROSCOPES WRITTEN ON YOUR PHONE
• Daily readings and in-depth natal readings are written by the AI model built into supported phones (Gemini Nano). Your birth details and readings are never sent to a cloud AI.
• On phones without on-device AI you still get a full reading, assembled from a hand-written library.

CHINESE ZODIAC, FOUR PILLARS AND BLOOD TYPE
• Your year animal and element, with the correct solar-term boundary, plus the lunar-new-year animal.
• Your Four Pillars of Destiny (BaZi): year, month, day and hour pillars.
• Add your blood type (A, B, AB or O) for the East-Asian blood-type personality reading, and get a combined Eastern reading.

PALM READING
• Hold your open hand inside the guide and take a photo. Conjunction measures your hand shape and your heart, head, life and fate lines on the phone, and writes your palm reading from them.
• Your palm photo is never uploaded, and it is deleted after the reading unless you save it.

CONJUNCTIONS NEARBY (OPTIONAL, 18+)
• Nearby is off until you turn it on. While it is on, Conjunction shows a notification so you always know it is running, and you can pause it for an hour or until tomorrow.
• When another member who also opted in is within about 50 metres and your intentions fit (dating or friends, and who each of you is looking for), both phones light up: your two zodiac signs fly in and merge into one emblem, and your compatibility score counts up.
• Compatibility blends Western synastry (Sun, Moon, Venus, Mars and Rising), Chinese zodiac harmony and clashes, and blood-type pairing.
• Born on the same day? Birthday twins get their own lavish celebration.
• Swipe right to accept or left to pass. When you both swipe right, it's a Conjunction: your chat opens.
• Your exact location is never shown to anyone. Other members only ever see "nearby", and our servers keep only your latest position for 10 minutes, with no location history.

SAFETY AND PRIVACY
• For adults 18 and over only.
• Profile photos are reviewed before anyone sees them, and every chat message is checked by automated moderation before it is delivered.
• Block, unmatch or report anyone from their card or from the chat. Reports are reviewed by a person.
• Your birth time and place, readings and palm photos stay on your phone. Delete your account and all its data at any time from Settings.
• No ads. No selling of your data.

Readings and compatibility scores are for entertainment. They are not advice, and they are not a substitute for your own judgement about the people you meet. Please read our safety tips before meeting anyone in person.
