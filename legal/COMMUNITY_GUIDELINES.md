Conjunction introduces people who happen to be near each other, for dating or friendship, by way of the
stars. That only works if everyone feels safe and respected. These guidelines are part of our Terms of
Service. They apply to your profile (name, photo, bio, dating and friends settings), your chats, and how
you treat people you meet through the App, in and out of the App.

## The short version

**Be real. Be kind. Respect "no". Keep it safe. Keep it legal.**

## 1. Be real

- **Adults only.** You must be 18 or over. Never ask for or encourage contact with minors. The App
  refuses cards that show an age under 18, and we report child sexual exploitation to the authorities
  (in the US, to NCMEC).
- **Be yourself.** Use your own first name and recent photos of you. No impersonation, fake profiles,
  celebrity photos, AI-generated faces passed off as you, or profiles for someone else.
- **Tell the truth about what matters.** Your age, gender, relationship status and what you are looking
  for must be accurate. If you are in a relationship, do not set "single" to get dating matches.
- **One profile per person.** No duplicate or shared profiles, and no reinstalling or using another
  phone to get around a block, a pass or a report.
- **Do not fake being nearby.** Relaying, replaying or spoofing Bluetooth signals, modified or imitation
  apps, emulators and bots are not allowed.

## Child safety

**Conjunction has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).** It is forbidden to
use the App to create, share, request or link to child sexual abuse material; to groom, sexualise,
solicit or arrange to meet anyone under 18; or to traffic or exploit a child in any way. Anyone under
18 must not use the App.

- **Report it** from the App (Report → "Child safety / possible minor"), or email [CONTACT EMAIL]
  with "Child safety" in the subject. If a child is in immediate danger, call emergency services
  first.
- **What we do:** we review child-safety reports first, preserve the information we received, and
  report apparent child sexual abuse material and exploitation to the US National Center for Missing
  & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline or the relevant authority in other countries, and
  cooperate with law enforcement. Because chats are end-to-end encrypted between phones, we only see
  what is sent to us in a report.
- Child-safety contact: [CHILD SAFETY CONTACT NAME], [CONTACT EMAIL].

## 2. Be kind

- **No harassment or bullying.** No insults, threats, intimidation, repeated unwanted messages, or
  pressuring someone after they have said no or gone quiet.
- **No hate.** No content that attacks or demeans people for race, ethnicity, national origin, caste,
  religion, disability, disease, age, sex, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, or serious
  illness. No hate symbols or praise of hate groups.
- **No body-shaming or star-shaming.** Disliking someone's sign is fine; using it to demean them is not.

## 3. Respect "no"

- A left swipe, an unmatch, a block or silence is an answer. **Do not try to contact someone by other
  means after they have declined, unmatched or blocked you**, and do not reinstall the App or use another
  phone to reach them.
- A match, a right swipe, a compatibility score or a birthday-twin popup is **not consent** to anything.
- **Never** follow, wait for, or try to locate someone you were matched with. Using the App, or its
  Bluetooth signals, to find or track a person is forbidden and may be reported to the police.

## 4. Keep it appropriate

- **No nudity or sexual content** in photos or bios. No sexual messages unless the other person has
  clearly welcomed them, and **never unsolicited sexual images**.
- **No graphic violence, gore or self-harm content.** If someone mentions self-harm, encourage them to
  contact emergency services or a crisis line. If you think they are in immediate danger, call
  emergency services.
- **No sharing of other people's private information or images**, including screenshots of someone's
  card, profile or chat, without their consent (sending them to us in a report, or to the police, is
  fine). Never share intimate images of anyone without consent.

## 5. No scams, selling or spam

- **No asking for money, gifts, cryptocurrency, investments or financial details.**
- **No commercial solicitation, sex work solicitation, escort services or advertising.**
- **No links to malware or phishing**, and no "move to this other app" lures designed to get around our
  safety tools.
- No promotion of illegal drugs or weapons sales.

## 6. Keep it legal

- Follow the law wherever you are. No content or conduct that is illegal, including threats, stalking,
  human trafficking, or exploitation of anyone.
- Respect intellectual property: post only photos and words you have the right to use.

## 7. Readings are entertainment

- Readings are written by AI on your phone and are for fun only. Do not use them to pressure, diagnose,
  insult or make claims about other people. If a reading is offensive or upsetting, report it.

## How we enforce

Conjunction has **no servers and no accounts**: your card and your chats go directly between phones
over Bluetooth, and chats are end-to-end encrypted. That protects your privacy, and it also means we
cannot read chats, remove content from anyone's phone or ban an account. Enforcement therefore works
like this:

- **Screening on the receiving phone.** The App screens photos and chat messages **on the phone that
  receives them**, with the phone's on-device AI. Content that may break these guidelines is hidden
  behind a warning or blurred; you decide whether to look. Screening is automated and makes mistakes.
- **No messages before a mutual match.** Nobody can message you unless you both swiped right.
- **Your tools work instantly on your phone** (below): block, unmatch, snooze, pause.
- **Reports come to a person.** Report (on every match card, friend and chat) blocks the person and
  prepares an email to [CONTACT EMAIL] with their card and, if you choose, the messages. We aim to
  respond within 24 hours and prioritise safety, threats and minors. We pass threats to life and
  child sexual exploitation to the police (in the US, NCMEC) and help you with next steps.
  [Engineering/Owner: if implemented, confirmed abusers' identity keys are added to a blocked-keys
  list shipped in App updates, so updated Apps refuse to match or chat with them.]
- **Appeals and questions.** If you think we got something wrong, email [CONTACT EMAIL] within 6
  months. A person will review it.

## Your tools

- **Block**: on your phone, ends the chat, removes the friend, hides their card and stops future
  matches and messages from that person.
- **Unmatch**: ends the chat and removes the friend on your phone. Their phone keeps its own copy.
- **Report**: blocks, and tells us about the problem by email.
- **Snooze**: stops match notifications about one person for a while.
- **Pause Nearby**: 1 hour or until tomorrow; or turn Nearby off.

Please also read the **Safety Tips** before meeting anyone.
