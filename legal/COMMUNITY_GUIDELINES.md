Conjunction introduces people who happen to be near each other, for dating or friendship, by way of the
stars. That only works if everyone feels safe and respected. These guidelines are part of our Terms of
Service. They apply to your profile (name, photo, bio, dating and friends settings), your chats, and how
you treat people you meet through the App, in and out of the App.

## The short version

**Be real. Be kind. Respect "no". Keep it safe. Keep it legal.**

## 1. Be real

- **Adults only.** You must be 18 or over. Never ask for or encourage contact with minors. We remove
  accounts of anyone under 18 and report child sexual exploitation to the authorities (in the US, to
  NCMEC).
- **Be yourself.** Use your own first name and recent photos of you. No impersonation, fake profiles,
  celebrity photos, AI-generated faces passed off as you, or accounts for someone else.
- **Tell the truth about what matters.** Your age, gender, relationship status and what you are looking
  for must be accurate. If you are in a relationship, do not set "single" to get dating matches.
- **One account per person.** No duplicate, shared or sold accounts, and no new account after a ban.
- **Do not fake your location.** Location spoofing, emulators and bots are not allowed.

## 2. Be kind

- **No harassment or bullying.** No insults, threats, intimidation, repeated unwanted messages, or
  pressuring someone after they have said no or gone quiet.
- **No hate.** No content that attacks or demeans people for race, ethnicity, national origin, caste,
  religion, disability, disease, age, sex, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, or serious
  illness. No hate symbols or praise of hate groups.
- **No body-shaming or star-shaming.** Disliking someone's sign is fine; using it to demean them is not.

## 3. Respect "no"

- A left swipe, an unmatch, a block or silence is an answer. **Do not try to contact someone by other
  means after they have declined, unmatched or blocked you**, and do not create new accounts to reach
  them.
- A match, a right swipe, a compatibility score or a birthday-twin popup is **not consent** to anything.
- **Never** follow, wait for, or try to locate someone you were matched with. Using the App to find or
  track a person is grounds for an immediate ban and may be reported to the police.

## 4. Keep it appropriate

- **No nudity or sexual content** in photos or bios. No sexual messages unless the other person has
  clearly welcomed them, and **never unsolicited sexual images**.
- **No graphic violence, gore or self-harm content.** If someone mentions self-harm, encourage them to
  contact emergency services or a crisis line and report the conversation so we can check on it.
- **No sharing of other people's private information or images**, including screenshots of someone's
  profile or chat, without their consent. Never share intimate images of anyone without consent.

## 5. No scams, selling or spam

- **No asking for money, gifts, cryptocurrency, investments or financial details.**
- **No commercial solicitation, sex work solicitation, escort services or advertising.**
- **No links to malware or phishing**, and no "move to this other app" lures designed to get around our
  safety tools.
- No promotion of illegal drugs or weapons sales.

## 6. Keep it legal

- Follow the law wherever you are. No content or conduct that is illegal, including threats, stalking,
  human trafficking, or exploitation of anyone.
- Respect intellectual property: post only photos and words you have the right to use.

## 7. Readings are entertainment

- Readings are written by AI on your phone and are for fun only. Do not use them to pressure, diagnose,
  insult or make claims about other people. If a reading is offensive or upsetting, report it.

## How we enforce

- **Automated checks before anyone sees your content.** Names, bios and every chat message are checked
  by automated text moderation, and photos by automated image moderation, before other users see them.
  Content that is clearly against these guidelines is blocked; content the systems are unsure about is
  held for a person to review. Until your photo is approved, your match card shows your zodiac art.
- **Reports.** Anyone can report a profile, photo, chat message or reading from inside the App. Reports
  are confidential. We prioritise reports about safety, threats and minors.
- **Actions.** Depending on how serious it is, we may remove content, give a warning, limit features
  (for example pause your Nearby access or chats), suspend your account, or ban you permanently. Serious
  violations, such as threats, sexual exploitation, involving minors, or stalking, lead to an immediate
  ban, and we may report them to the police.
- **Appeals.** If you think we got it wrong, email [CONTACT EMAIL] within 6 months with your account ID
  (Settings → Account). A person will review it.

## Your tools

- **Block**: stops all matches and messages between you and the other person, ends any chat, and removes
  your profile from their view.
- **Unmatch**: ends the chat for both of you.
- **Report**: tells us about a problem; you can also block at the same time.
- **Snooze**: stops match notifications about one person for a while.
- **Pause Nearby**: 1 hour or until tomorrow; or turn Nearby off.

Please also read the **Safety Tips** before meeting anyone.
